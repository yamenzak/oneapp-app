# Notifications

**OneLegal sends nothing**, and there is a better mechanism in its place.

A version change does not mail anybody. It raises the gate: the next time a
person or a workspace owner makes a request, `gate.outstanding` answers that
something is owed and the shell shows it before the product. An email saying
"our terms have changed" is a thing people archive; a screen between them and
their work is a thing they read.

That is a deliberate trade and it has a cost worth naming: somebody who does
not sign in never learns the terms changed. For a workspace-audience document
that is acceptable, because the acceptance is the organisation's and the owner
signs in; for a person-audience document, the person who never signs in is a
person whose data we are not handling differently anyway.

## What would be worth sending

One thing, and it is not built: telling a **workspace owner** that a material
revision is coming, before it lands, so an organisation with a review process
has time to run it. That is a mail from the control plane about a tenant rather
than a rule on a tenant site, which is why it is not an `ALERTS` row here.
